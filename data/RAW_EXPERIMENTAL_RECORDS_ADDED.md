# Additional raw experimental records

This release preserves the existing repository data and adds the latest author-supplied raw records for: (1) five-transducer ultrasonic calibration, (2) incidence-angle response, (3) target-material response, (4) simultaneous-firing cross-talk, (5) ten-case fusion-weight sensitivity, (6) trial-level paired subsystem ablation, and (7) a continuous Smart Stick discharge run.

The records were supplied for integration into the release; this update does not independently witness or reproduce the physical experiments. Derived coefficients and summaries are computed from the supplied rows. The manuscript uses measured/author-supplied wording accordingly and does not claim independent verification by the release editor.
